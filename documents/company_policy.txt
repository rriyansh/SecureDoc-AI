Company Policy: Data Retention and Access Control

1. Data Retention
All customer data must be retained for a minimum of 3 years and a maximum of 7 years.
After 7 years, data must be securely deleted in accordance with the data destruction policy.
Retention periods may be extended only with written approval from the Chief Data Officer.

2. Access Control
Access to customer data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
All access must be logged and audited quarterly.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems containing customer data.

3. Incident Response
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.
The security team will initiate the incident response plan and notify affected parties
in accordance with applicable regulations.

4. Data Classification
Customer data is classified as "Confidential" and must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
